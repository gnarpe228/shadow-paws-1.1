Shadow Paws

You are a small black cat on an endless walk. The world keeps unfolding: meadows, pine forests, autumn woods, desert, snow, jungle, lakes, towns, rocky hills with mountains, and crystal caves full of ores. Sneak up on birds, and make friends with the cats you meet along the way.

Controls
  • W A S D walk, Shift run, Space jump
  • E catch a bird, befriend a cat, collect crystals and ores
  • Drag or Arrow keys look around, Wheel zoom
  • V first / third person
  • Q inventory, M biome map, B badges
  • N notes, C take a photo
  • R rest, R+E sleep 6 hours
  • F meow: scares birds off, calls cats over (10 m)
  • Z line your friends up straight behind you
  • 1–4 pick a tool, G or click to use it; hold Space to keep jumping
  • K or tap the clock: calendar
  • X set a TP point, then tap it in TP points to teleport back
  • T or tap the chat box: open the friend chat, tap again to hide it
  • Tap a friend in the inventory to find it in your line
  • Esc pause
  • On a phone, switch on Mobile controls. Hold R and tap the action button to sleep 6 hours.
